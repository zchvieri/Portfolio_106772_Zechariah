🌱 Systems and Security

Green and Energy-Efficient Data Centre Management

A system management report on Calibarn Data Centre Sdn Bhd, a colocation data centre near Kuching that hosts servers for about 50 business and government clients. The centre faces rising electricity costs, cooling systems more than ten years old, and clients who ask for greener operations. My part of the report looks at how these problems can be managed.

Type: Report assignment (case study)

🔒 Systems and Security

Password Guessing Attack: System Architecture and Security

A group presentation on how password guessing attacks work and how a system protects against them. It covers password protection and authentication layers, multi-factor authentication (MFA), AI/ML-based suspicious login detection, and mitigation and response. I explained the system architecture, and our group divided the presentation parts between members.

Type: Group project (presentation)
